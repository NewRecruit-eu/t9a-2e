# The Ninth Age: Fantasy Battles – 2nd Edition

BattleScribe / New Recruit data for **The 9th Age: Fantasy Battles, 2nd edition** (2024 army books,
2023 rulebook): the 16 army books and the 7 supplement armies (Åsklanders, Cultists, Dread Fleet,
Cult of Errahman, Hobgoblins, Legions of Sin, Makhar).

- Checked against the official 2nd-edition PDFs: the PDF always wins.
- Warbands (3000 pts or less) and Grand Armies (8000 pts or more) scale the "0–X per Army" limits
  automatically from the list's points limit.
- In the 2nd edition spells are chosen at the start of the game: the builder only shows the spells of a
  Path, there is no spell to pick in the list.

For the current edition, see the separate "The 9th Age" system.
